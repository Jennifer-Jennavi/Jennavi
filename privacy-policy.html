What we do with your data, plainly

Jennavi ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. Here's exactly what we collect, how we use it, and what we simply never do.

Our Commitments, At a Glance

What We Do

  • Collect only what's needed to deliver our service
  • Use PCI-compliant third-party processors for payment
  • Let you request access, correction, or deletion anytime

What We Never Do

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Store your full card details on our own servers
  • Share your data beyond what's needed to operate

Full detail on every point below ↓

01 — Collection

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide directly, such as your name and email address, when filling out a form, booking a call, or making a payment
  • Payment information, processed securely by our third-party payment processors (Flutterwave and/or Axra). We do not store your card details on our own servers
  • Usage data, collected automatically via Google Analytics, including pages visited, time on site, and general location (country/city level)
  • Communications you send us via WhatsApp or email
02 — Usage

How We Use It

  • To deliver the services you've purchased or inquired about
  • To respond to your messages and inquiries
  • To send confirmation emails related to a purchase or booking
  • To understand how visitors use our site, so we can improve it
03 — Third Parties

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We share necessary information only with the service providers required to operate our business, such as our payment processors, scheduling tools, and email delivery services, solely to the extent needed to provide our services.

04 — Security

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Payment processing is handled entirely by PCI-compliant third-party processors; Jennavi never directly stores or has access to your full card details.

05 — Your Control

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us directly.

Questions About This Policy?

Reach out directly, we'll walk you through anything that's unclear.
