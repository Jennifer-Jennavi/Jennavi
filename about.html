About Jennavi

The Story Behind Jennavi

Jennavi is a premium LinkedIn ghostwriting agency founded and personally operated by Jennifer Omaliko. We help startup founders, CEOs, and consultants, primarily across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia, turn LinkedIn into a genuine source of visibility, trust, and inbound opportunity.

Before taking on a single client, Jennifer proved the entire methodology on her own LinkedIn profile first. The result: 873% impression growth, 6,000+ organically built followers, and 106,000+ organic impressions, all without a single dollar spent on ads. Every result is documented and verifiable.

What We Do

Jennavi provides done-for-you LinkedIn content strategy and ghostwriting for founders and executives who know their presence matters but don't have the time, or the specific skill, to build it themselves. Every word published under a client's name is written personally by Jennifer. Nothing is outsourced.

Our Approach

  • Voice-first positioning, not generic templated content
  • A documented, repeatable methodology (CRICKETS) behind every post
  • Direct, one-on-one work with Jennifer, no account managers, no ghostwriting teams
  • A service agreement in place before any work begins

Where We Work

Jennavi operates fully remotely from Kano, Nigeria, serving clients globally, with a primary focus on founders and executives in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.
