The Short Answer

How does LinkedIn lead generation
work at Jennavi?

Your profile and content come first, because people check them before they reply to anyone. Then we find the right people by hand, let them see your name, comment on their posts, connect with them personally and start relevant conversations. When someone shows genuine interest, we hand the conversation to you with a suggested reply. Every month we report what happened against the numbers we recorded on day one.

Phase 1 · Steps 1 to 3

Foundation: your profile,
voice and content.

Your profile is where people decide. Before we contact anyone, we make sure the right visitor finds a credible expert worth talking to, speaking in your own voice.

01

Foundation

Profile optimization

What we do. We rebuild your headline so it says who you help, your About section so it tells your story with proof, and your banner and Featured section so they show your best work.

What you do. You tell us who you want to reach and what you want to be known for.

What you receive. A profile that gives the right visitor a reason to stay and one clear way to reach you. This happens in week one.

02

Foundation

Voice development

What we do. We learn how you speak, agree who you want to reach and capture both in a voice guide.

What you do. You review and approve the voice guide, and nothing is published before you do.

What you receive. An approved voice guide and a clearly defined ideal client.

03

Foundation

Ghostwritten content

What we do. We write posts from your real stories, decisions and opinions. The Founder tier includes 12 posts a month, and the Executive tier includes 16 posts plus a monthly newsletter and SEO and GEO article.

What you do. You share the experiences and opinions that your posts draw on.

What you receive. A steady presence in your own voice that builds trust and supports every later step.

To go deeper on the first step, read about LinkedIn profile optimization and LinkedIn personal branding.

Phase 2 · Steps 4 to 8

Outreach: the right people,
reached thoughtfully.

Outreach starts only after the foundation is ready. We do not rely on mass messaging. We find the right people by hand, let them get to know your name and lead with relevant conversations instead of sales pitches.

04

Outreach

Prospect identification

What we do. We search LinkedIn, groups, events and the people who engage with relevant posts, then remove poor fits before anyone is contacted. At the start we also check that enough of your buyers can be found.

What you do. You tell us who your best clients are.

What you receive. A hand-built list of the right people.

05

Outreach

Audience warm-up

What we do. We follow and view the profiles of the right people, so your name is familiar before any message arrives.

What you do. You can carry on with your day while this happens.

What you receive. An audience that has already seen your name.

06

Outreach

Thoughtful commenting

What we do. We leave thoughtful comments on their posts, written in your voice, and we review every comment before it is posted.

What you do. There is nothing for you to write, because each comment is drafted and reviewed for you.

What you receive. Visibility with the right people, earned through substance and not through volume.

07

Outreach

Personalized connection requests

What we do. Each request refers to something real in the person's profile or content, never a template, and goes out at a measured and steady pace.

What you do. There is nothing for you to send, because each request is written around the person we are contacting.

What you receive. Accepted connections from people who already know who you are.

08

Outreach

Relevant conversations

What we do. Once a connection is accepted, a short and useful message opens the conversation. Follow-ups are spaced out and stop the moment someone replies, and we lead with relevant conversations instead of sales pitches.

What you do. You can stay focused on your work until someone shows real interest.

What you receive. Real conversations with the right people.

Phase 3 · Steps 9 and 10

Results: warm handoffs
and honest reporting.

When someone is interested, you hear about it. Every month you can also see what happened, measured against a real baseline.

09

Results

Interested-prospect handoff

What we do. We track replies in a CRM. When a prospect shows genuine interest, we send you a heads-up, the conversation so far and a suggested reply.

What you do. You review and approve the suggested reply, then take the call.

What you receive. Warm leads who are ready to book.

10

Results

Monthly reporting

What we do. We record your starting numbers on day one, report every month and refine targeting and messages based on what is working.

What you do. You review the report and the plan for the following month.

What you receive. New connections, replies, positive replies, calls booked, and post and profile views, shown against your starting numbers.

Before You Book

Questions about how
the process works

How does LinkedIn lead generation work at Jennavi?

Jennavi runs a ten-step process in three phases. In the Foundation phase we optimize your profile, build your voice guide and publish ghostwritten content. In the Outreach phase we identify prospects by hand, warm them up, comment thoughtfully, send personalized connection requests and hold relevant conversations. In the Results phase we hand off interested prospects and report every month. The order never changes, and outreach starts only after the foundation is ready.

Why do you optimize the profile and write content before starting outreach?

People check your profile and your posts before they reply to a message. If your profile does not say who you help and your content does not show how you think, outreach has nothing to land on. Clear positioning does much of the selling, so we build the foundation first and begin outreach only when it is ready.

What happens before the work starts?

You book a free strategy call and tell us your goal. Every client then signs a service agreement before work begins, covering scope, deliverables, revision policy, payment terms and confidentiality, and starts the paid 30-day pilot. The Founder pilot costs $500 and the Executive pilot costs $1,000.

What will I need to do?

You build the voice guide with us and approve it before anything is published. When a prospect shows genuine interest, you receive the conversation so far and a suggested reply to review and approve. The research, writing, engagement, outreach and tracking are handled for you.

How do you find the right prospects?

We build every list by hand. We search LinkedIn, groups and events, and we look at the people who engage with relevant posts. Poor fits are removed before anyone is contacted, and at the start we check that enough of your buyers can be found.

Do you send template messages or use mass outreach?

No. Each connection request refers to something real in the person's profile, never a template. Outreach runs at a measured and steady pace, follow-ups stop the moment someone replies, and we never use mass outreach or buy followers.

What happens when a prospect replies?

Replies are tracked in a CRM. When a prospect shows genuine interest, you receive a heads-up, the conversation so far and a suggested reply to review and approve, so warm leads reach you ready to book as calls.

What is in the monthly report?

Every month you see new connections, replies, positive replies, calls booked, and post and profile views. We record your starting numbers on day one, so each report shows a real before and after, and we use the results to refine targeting and messages.

What does the first 30 days look like?

Week one is setup, covering your profile, voice guide, ideal client list and starting numbers. In week two your posts go live and we begin following and commenting. In week three connection notes go out and the first messages follow. In week four you receive your first report and a plan for month two.

How long does it take to see results?

LinkedIn is not always predictable. Results vary and may take one to three months to appear, and we never promise leads or revenue because we do not control LinkedIn's rules, who sees each post or your market.

What if my buyers are hard to find on LinkedIn?

We check at the start that enough of your buyers can be found. We learned this from a client in a very narrow niche whose buyers large databases mostly missed, so we now build every list by hand instead of relying on a database.

Can I stop after the pilot?

Yes. The pilot is a single paid month, and afterwards you continue month to month or commit for three or six months to save. There is no long-term lock-in, and a longer commitment changes the price but never the service.
