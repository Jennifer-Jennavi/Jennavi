What is the difference between Hook Audit and Content Audit?

Hook Audit scores only your opening line across four CRICKETS hook dimensions. Use it before writing the full post to test whether your hook stops the scroll. Content Audit scores your full post across Hook Quality, Pain Specificity, Proof Presence, Conversion Intent, plus length and formatting. The hook determines whether anyone reads the rest. The content determines whether anyone reaches out after reading.
