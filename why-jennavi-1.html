The Jennavi Sequence

From a profile worth visiting
to a call worth taking.

Every engagement follows the same order. First we make your profile worth visiting, then we build trust with content in your voice, and only then does outreach begin, at a safe and steady pace. The campaign works because the foundation is already in place.

Step 1 · Foundation

Optimize your profile

Your headline, About section, banner and Featured section are rebuilt, so every visitor sees a credible expert worth talking to and a clear path to your website.

Step 2 · Foundation

Define your audience and voice

We agree who you want to reach and capture how you speak in a voice guide that you approve before anything is published.

Step 3 · Foundation

Publish in your voice

Posts written in your voice go live every month, 12 on the Founder tier and 16 on the Executive tier, and they keep building trust throughout every later step.

Step 4 · Outreach

Choose the right people

We build a prospect list from LinkedIn search, post engagers, groups and events, then filter out poor fits before anyone is contacted.

Step 5 · Outreach

Warm them up

We follow selected prospects and view their profiles, so your name is familiar to them before any message arrives.

Step 6 · Outreach

Engage with substance

We leave thoughtful comments on their posts, written in your voice and reviewed before they go out.

Step 7 · Outreach

Connect personally

A connection request goes out that refers to something real in their profile or content, never a generic template.

Step 8 · Outreach

Start the conversation

Once a connection is accepted, a short, value-led message opens the conversation, followed by spaced follow-ups that stop the moment they reply.

Step 9 · Results

Hand off warm leads

Replies are tracked in a CRM and warm leads are handed to you ready to book as calls.

Step 10 · Results

Report and refine

Each month you receive your accepted connections, positive replies and booked calls, and we adjust targeting and messages based on what is working.

Before You Decide

Founders
ask this

I've been burned by a ghostwriter before. How is this different?

Most ghostwriters hand you content and hope it works. Jennifer starts with a positioning diagnosis before any writing begins, then pairs your content with outreach that puts it in front of the right buyers. If a previous ghostwriter delivered views without clients, the gap was usually the strategy underneath the writing. Jennifer builds your voice guide and reviews every piece of work herself. See the documented proof on the Jennavi home page, or read why an agency and an independent ghostwriter fail founders differently.

Will the content actually sound like me?

Every engagement starts with a voice capture session. Jennifer studies how you speak, what you care about and the language of your industry before any writing begins, and nothing is published until you approve your voice guide. Revisions are unlimited within scope until the content sounds like you.

How does Jennavi generate leads and not just posts?

We optimize your profile, define your ideal client and run targeted outreach campaigns that combine connection requests, thoughtful comments and follow-up messages. Your content builds trust while the campaigns put you in front of the right people. Replies are tracked and handed to you ready to book as calls.

Which tier is right for me?

The Founder tier suits you if you want steady inbound conversations from 12 posts a month, profile optimization and outreach campaigns. The Executive tier adds 16 posts, a monthly newsletter, an SEO article and priority support, and it suits leaders who are building a category presence. The free LinkedIn Positioning Health Check scores your profile and recommends a tier, and a strategy call gives you a direct recommendation.

What happens in the 30-day pilot?

You pay a reduced entry fee ($500 for Founder, $1,000 for Executive). In that month we complete your profile optimization, voice guide and target audience, launch content and campaigns, and report early results. After that, you continue on the monthly plan.

How much does it cost?

The Founder tier costs $1,000 per month and the Executive tier costs $1,500 per month, after a 30-day pilot at $500 or $1,000. Team savings of 5 to 12 percent apply for multiple profiles, commitments of 3 or 6 months save 5 or 10 percent, and all fees are in USD. The full breakdown is in the pricing section above.

Is it worth the price?

The better question is what an invisible LinkedIn profile costs you today. One client won through LinkedIn can cover many months of the service, and the pilot lets you test the system for one month before you commit further. The free LinkedIn ROI Calculator estimates what silence is costing you.

How quickly will I see results?

During the 30-day pilot you will see activity you can measure: accepted connections, replies and early conversations. Consistent inbound inquiries usually develop over the first few months as your new positioning compounds. Closed deals depend on your offer and your sales process, so we report what we control and measure.

Do you guarantee clients or revenue?

No, and we would be wary of anyone who does. We report accepted connections, positive replies and booked calls every month. What you close depends on your offer and your sales conversations.

What if I pay and it does not work for my business?

The pilot exists for this reason. Every engagement begins with a conversation to confirm a genuine fit, and the first month is a paid pilot, so you test the system before committing further. If your tier is not producing what was outlined, that is addressed directly and not brushed aside. See why Jennavi works.

I have never met Jennifer. How do I know this is legitimate?

Jennifer's results are public and verifiable: 873% impression growth on her own profile, a Google Business Profile with verified reviews, and the screenshots shown on the Jennavi home page. You can also read the full founder story.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No long-term lock-in. Every client signs a service agreement before work begins, covering scope, deliverables, revision policy, payment terms and confidentiality. The pilot is a single paid month, and after that you continue month to month or commit for 3 or 6 months for a saving.

I am based in the US, UK or Australia. Will the time difference be a problem?

No. Most Jennavi clients are in the US, UK, Australia and Canada. Jennifer works across time zones, and strategy calls are scheduled at times that suit yours.
