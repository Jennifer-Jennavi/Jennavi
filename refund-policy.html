We want every client to feel confident before committing to work with Jennavi. This policy outlines exactly when refunds are available, and why, across our service tiers.

01 — Retainers

Monthly Retainer Tiers · Starter, Growth, Authority, VIP, Executive

Change of Mind, Before Onboarding Begins

100%

Full refund, minus non-refundable payment processing fees (typically 3–5%, charged by our payment processor and not recoverable by us), provided your voice capture session has not yet taken place and no content drafting has begun.

Delayed Response or Client Inactivity

If you do not respond to outreach or schedule your voice capture session within 14 days of payment, and later request a refund, only 50% of your payment will be refunded. This reflects the planning, strategy time, and availability already committed on your behalf during that period, even though drafting had not yet begun.

After Onboarding or Content Drafting Has Begun

Once your voice capture session has been completed, or content drafting has started, the month's payment becomes non-refundable, as strategy time and writing work have already been committed on your behalf. You may still cancel at any time to prevent future billing; see our Terms & Conditions for cancellation details.

02 — One-Time

Taster Tier · One-Time Profile Audit

Because this is a one-time deliverable typically completed quickly after purchase, refunds are only available if the audit has not yet been started. Once delivered, the Taster tier is non-refundable.

03 — Digital Product

CRICKETS Manifesto · Book, $9.99

As a digital product delivered instantly upon purchase, the CRICKETS Manifesto is non-refundable once the download link has been sent, except in cases of a technical error preventing access, in which case contact us and we will resolve it promptly.

How to Request a Refund

If you believe you qualify under the terms above, message us directly, we'll walk you through it personally.
