I've been burned by a ghostwriter before. How is this any different?

Most ghostwriters hand you content and hope it works. Jennifer starts with a positioning diagnosis before writing a single word. If your previous ghostwriter gave you posts that got views but no clients, the problem wasn't the writing, it was the strategy underneath it. She writes every word personally. No junior writers, no outsourcing, no templates. See the documented results, or read why an agency and an independent ghostwriter fail founders differently.

Will the content actually sound like me, or will it be obvious someone else wrote it?

Every engagement starts with a deep voice capture session. Jennifer studies how you speak, what you care about, your industry language and your business goals before writing a single word. Founders consistently say the content sounds more like them than what they write themselves. Revisions are unlimited within scope until it does.

I'm not sure which tier is right for me. How do I decide?

Start with the free LinkedIn Positioning Health Check. It scores your profile and recommends a tier based on where you actually are. The free 30-minute strategy call is the best place to get a direct recommendation for your specific situation.

How quickly will I actually see results?

Most clients see meaningful impression growth and profile visit increases within the first 30 days. Consistent inbound inquiries typically develop between month 2 and month 4 as LinkedIn's algorithm learns the new positioning. Results are public and verifiable at jennavi.co/founder.html.

Is $1,000 a month really worth it for LinkedIn content?

The right question is what your invisible LinkedIn is costing you right now. One missed client from LinkedIn likely costs more than a full year of the Executive retainer. Use the free LinkedIn ROI Calculator to find out what silence is costing you.

I'm not sure I can justify spending on this right now. What if I'm wrong to invest?

That hesitation is normal, worth taking seriously rather than talking you out of. Almost every founder who reached out, showed real interest, then paused, traced it back to timing, not the investment itself once they saw their own numbers. Start with the free Health Check or the $100 Taster tier before deciding whether a larger tier makes sense.

What if I pay and it doesn't actually work for my specific business?

This is exactly why every engagement starts with a free diagnosis call before any payment, so both sides can confirm it's a genuine fit first.
