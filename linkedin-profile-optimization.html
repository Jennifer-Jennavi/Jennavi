What A Profile Has To Do

Every visitor asks four questions.
Your profile has seconds to answer them.

People open your profile after a message, a comment or a post. What they find decides whether the conversation continues. Each part of the profile answers one of these questions.

01

Who is this person?

Your headline and banner introduce you. They should say who you help in a single glance, in plain language.

02

Do they help people like me?

Your About section tells your story and shows proof. A visitor wants to see their own situation described accurately.

03

Can I trust them?

Your Featured section shows your best work. It gives a visitor the posts, pages and results you most want them to see.

04

What do I do next?

One clear way to reach you gives an interested visitor an obvious next step, instead of a search for how to get in touch.

Before You Book

Questions about
LinkedIn profile optimization

What is LinkedIn profile optimization?

LinkedIn profile optimization is the work of rewriting and rebuilding your profile so that the right visitor understands who you help, believes you and knows how to reach you. At Jennavi that means a new headline, an About section with your story and proof, a banner and Featured section that show your best work, and one clear way to contact you.

What exactly do you change on my profile?

We rebuild your headline, your About section, your banner and your Featured section, and we make sure there is one clear way for an interested visitor to reach you. Every change is written around the audience and voice guide that we build with you at the start.

How long does LinkedIn profile optimization take?

Profile optimization is delivered in week one of the 30-day pilot, alongside your voice guide, your ideal client list and your starting numbers. Posts and outreach begin only after that foundation is ready.

Will my profile sound like me?

Yes. Before we write anything, we learn how you speak and who you want to reach, and we capture both in a voice guide that you approve. Your headline and About section are then written in that voice, using your own story and proof.

Is this just a matter of adding keywords?

No. Keywords help people find a profile, but positioning is what persuades them once they arrive. We rebuild your profile around who you help and the problem you solve, so a visitor can decide quickly that you are the right person to talk to.

Can I buy profile optimization on its own?

Profile optimization is included in every Jennavi engagement as the first of ten steps, delivered in week one. If you want to see where your profile stands before you commit, you can request a free LinkedIn profile audit, and Jennifer will explain in plain language why your profile earns views but not replies.

What does it cost?

Profile optimization is included in both tiers. The Founder tier costs $500 for the first 30 days and then $1,000 a month, and the Executive tier costs $1,000 for the first 30 days and then $1,500 a month. All fees are in USD, and a paid 30-day pilot comes before any longer commitment.

How will I know the new profile is working?

We record your starting numbers on day one, including your profile views, and report every month. You see new connections, replies, positive replies, calls booked, and post and profile views measured against that baseline.

Why not stop at the profile and skip posting and outreach?

A better profile helps every visit convert, but it still needs visits. Posts, thoughtful comments and personalized outreach bring the right people to your profile. That is why profile optimization is the first of ten steps and not the whole service.

What do I need to provide?

You tell us who you want to reach and what you want LinkedIn to do for you, and you approve your voice guide. We use that, together with your story and the proof you share, to write and build everything else.

Do you guarantee more clients from a better profile?

No. We do not own LinkedIn and cannot control its rules, who sees your profile or your market, so we never promise clients or revenue. We control the order of the work, the weekly effort and the monthly report.

Where do you work, and who do you serve?

The team works remotely, in English, from Kano, Nigeria, with founders, executives, consultants and sales teams in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and other markets.
