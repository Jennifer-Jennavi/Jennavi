My profile gets views but nobody reaches out. Will this tool tell me why?

Yes. The Full Profile Audit scores your Headline, About Section, and Experience against what actually converts a visitor into a conversation, not what makes a resume look complete. Most founders scoring below 50 have a headline that reads like a job title and an About section with no call to action at all. If you want Jennifer to fix it permanently, view all six service tiers here.

What is the difference between the three audit modes?

Profile Audit scores your full PDF export across three dimensions: Headline, About Section, and Experience. Headline Audit and About Section Audit let you test one section in isolation before rewriting it, useful if you already know which part is weak and want a sharper diagnosis of exactly why.

Is my LinkedIn PDF safe? Does it leave my browser?

No. Your PDF is read and scored entirely inside your own browser. It is never uploaded to a server or stored anywhere. Once you close the tab, it is gone.

What is a good LinkedIn profile score and what does mine mean?

81 to 100 means your profile is converting well. 61 to 80 means good but incomplete, missing one or two key elements. 41 to 60 means average, building awareness but not pipeline. 21 to 40 means significant repositioning needed. 0 to 20 means a full rebuild. Whatever your score, book a free 30-minute call with Jennifer.

My headline scored low. What is actually wrong with it?

Most low-scoring headlines fail the same test: they describe a job title instead of naming who you help and the outcome you deliver. The Headline Audit scores yours across four specific dimensions and tells you exactly which one is missing.

What is profile optimization and why does it matter?

Profile optimization means rewriting your headline, About section, and experience so the profile does not just describe you, it converts visitors into leads. Brilliant content goes nowhere if the profile behind it does not close the loop.
